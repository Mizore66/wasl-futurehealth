# heldout3 - third independent synthetic colonoscopy corpus

Generated by `gen_heldout3.py` (Python 3 stdlib only, seed 3031, no LLM, no network). Written from the task brief alone, without reading the other corpora or the Wasl code.

300 reports, unique texts. Truth: adequate 120, awaiting_histology 90, inadequate 90.
Key state: closed/criteria_met 67, needs_human/missing_documentation 112, not_closed/awaiting_histology 52, not_closed/inadequate_prep 35, not_closed/incomplete_exam 34.

Reports with at least one `hard:*` tag: 116 (39%).

## How it works

Each report gets a hidden reality (caecum reached, prep adequate, tissue taken, histology back) and a documentation plan (what the text states). Truth comes from reality, the key from the text. Items left unstated mostly hide a fine procedure, but about one in five hides a failure (tag `hidden:undocumented_failure`).

## Layouts and overlays

- `fmt:kv` software export with key: value lines; `fmt:headed` headed prose sections; `fmt:dictation` narrative dictation; `fmt:terse` abbreviated one-liners; `fmt:table` markdown/pipe table; `fmt:form` structured checkbox form (three glyph styles).
- Section order shuffled (sedation/prep/impression/withdrawal moves); label wording and case vary.
- `spelling:us` vs `spelling:uk`; `lang:arabic` (bilingual headings, Arabic summary block, or Arabic title + signature); `typo` (misspelt key words such as "ceacum", "polpy"); `noise:scan` (OCR letter swaps on non-key words, hyphenated line breaks, stray marks, scan/fax banners).
- Prep: verbal grades, Boston in 8 formats (incl. L/T/R order, multi-line, total-only), Aronchick with verbal grade, or ungraded (product only, compliance, "see nursing notes", blank field, omitted).
- Tissue: cold/hot snare, cold forceps, EMR; non-polyp biopsies (random for diarrhoea, rectal ulcer, sigmoid erythema, descending nodule, transverse colitis, terminal ileal aphthae); pathology appended in 4 formats.

## Keying decisions

- A caecal polyp or "appendiceal orifice normal" with no extent statement is keyed caecum=yes (necessarily implies it).
- Withdrawal time, "insertion uneventful", "normal colonoscopy" or an endoscopist audit rate alone are caecum=not_stated.
- Boston total-only is used only for totals 9, 8 or < 6, where segment rule is decidable.
- Corrections in addenda override the body; the key follows the corrected statement.
- Diminutive hyperplastic-appearing polyps left in situ are the only unremoved polyps; nothing large is deferred, no malignancy results.
- Every appended pathology result covers all specimens of the procedure.
