# Synthetic Abu Dhabi colonoscopy reports — dataset card

Author: independent author (GPT-6.1 Sol, OpenAI)

Publication date: 2026-10-29 (a fixed literal, not a clock reading). Version: heldout5, seed 5051.

## Purpose and provenance

This is a held-out test corpus of 300 wholly synthetic colonoscopy reports with answer keys. It tests reading of completion, preparation quality, tissue acquisition, and current histology. It contains no patient records, copied reports, network-derived material, or external reference files. Patient identifiers and location-based facility labels are synthetic; facility labels do not imply participation or endorsement by any provider. All reports describe Abu Dhabi day-surgery or hospital endoscopy services.

The author worked only in the current directory. The generator uses the Python 3 standard library, a local `random.Random(5051)` instance, fixed date literals, and authored scenario rules. It reads no input data, uses no network or LLM calls, and never reads a clock. Output is UTF-8 JSON with literal Arabic, two-space indentation, and one trailing newline.

## Files and reproduction

- `gen_heldout5.py`: executable generator and built-in validation, with hidden realities defined in `CASES`.
- `reports_heldout5.json`: the generated reports and answer key.
- `README.md`: this dataset card; it is not regenerated by the script.

Run from this directory:

```sh
python3 gen_heldout5.py
```

The script writes `reports_heldout5.json` beside itself, including when invoked from another working directory. It overwrites only that output. The run prints the truth counts and output SHA-256. No third-party packages are needed. There are no absolute paths embedded in these files.

## Generation and independent keying

Thirty authored clinical scenarios appear ten times each, shuffled with the fixed seed. Twelve scenarios represent adequate hidden reality, nine awaiting histology, and nine inadequate. Eight layouts are interleaved across the shuffled scenarios. Seeded choices vary units, age, sex, indication, sedation, clinical phrasing, preparation grade, Boston presentation, lesion size, lesion site, and administrative identifiers. All 300 full texts and SHA-256 hashes are unique. They are template-derived variations, not 300 independent clinical scenario designs.

Arabic overlays (60), mild scan/OCR overlays (50), and optional prior-procedure blocks (60) are sampled independently. An additional dedicated prior-histology scenario increases the prior-procedure union to 69 reports. Spelling is independently assigned to 150 UK and 150 US reports; facility proper names are retained. Arabic paragraphs repeat final documentary facts, omitting any fact missing from the English record; they never reveal hidden reality. OCR artefacts affect administrative words, spacing, headers, and footers, preserving clinical qualifiers and Boston numerals.

Each scenario separately declares four latent booleans (caecum reached, prep adequate, tissue taken, current histology back), documentary field values, and the expected key state/reason. Hidden reality is retained in the generator for audit but not included as an extra field in the output. `truth` is derived from latent facts; `key_fields` are authored from evidence in the rendered text, including later corrections. The two targets deliberately differ when documentation is missing. Tags and answer keys are evaluation metadata and must be withheld from any system being tested.

Truth precedence:

1. Failure to reach the caecum OR inadequate preparation gives `inadequate`.
2. Otherwise, tissue acquired without a current histology result gives `awaiting_histology`.
3. Otherwise, `adequate`.

The result-back latent flag is false whenever no tissue was taken. In incomplete procedures, any preparation assessment describes the examined bowel; truth is already inadequate because of incomplete extent.

## Additional authored hard-case families

- `hard:normal_mucosa_sampled` (30): Normal visual mucosa still has random mucosal biopsies.
- `hard:ileum_optional` (10): Failed terminal ileal intubation does not undo documented caecal landmarks.
- `hard:lesion_left_in_situ` (20): A seen polyp is deliberately left untouched, so lesion presence is not tissue acquisition.
- `hard:future_sampling` (10): A future biopsy or resection recommendation is not sampling during this procedure.
- `hard:post_lavage_grade` (10): The final post-wash quality assessment supersedes the dirty insertion view.
- `hard:outside_current_histology` (10): A current-procedure pathology result imported from another laboratory counts when linked by accession.
- `hard:regimen_not_quality` (50): Completing a preparation regimen says nothing about its observed cleansing adequacy.
- `hard:metrics_not_extent` (40): Withdrawal time and inspection photographs alone do not establish an insertion endpoint.
- `hard:resection_without_retrieval` (10): A polypectomy counts as tissue taken even when the specimen is lost and cannot yield histology.
- `hard:receipt_not_result` (10): Laboratory receipt and gross processing are not a microscopic histology result.
- `hard:other_site_histology` (10): Same-day gastric histology does not supply a result for the colon biopsies.
- `hard:fragments_not_procedures` (10): Several tissue fragments and containers still belong to one current sampling episode.
- `hard:segment_over_total` (10): A Boston total of seven fails adequacy when one segment is scored one.
- `hard:signed_result_unreleased` (10): A signed microscopic diagnosis counts even while the patient portal release is pending.
- `hard:failure_priority` (10): Documented incomplete extent takes priority over poor preparation and pending histology.
- `hard:extent_over_distance` (10): A long insertion distance and looping do not substitute for reaching the caecum.

The following three families implement the specifically requested prior-procedure and later-correction challenges; they are additional to the 16 families above.

- `hard:superseding_correction` (40): An explicitly superseding correction controls over an earlier signed or provisional statement.
- `hard:histology_addendum` (10): A later tissue diagnosis closes an earlier pending histology entry for the same accession.
- `hard:prior_episode` (69): Prior completion, preparation grades, and tissue diagnoses do not describe this procedure.

## Keying decisions requiring reviewer attention

- `caecum`: `yes`, `no`, or `not_stated`, reflecting the final current-procedure statement. A colonoscopy title, withdrawal time, archived photographs, ascending-colon lesion, insertion distance, or normal rectal examination does not by itself prove caecal intubation. Failure to enter the terminal ileum does not negate documented caecal completion.
- `prep`: `adequate`, `inadequate`, or `not_stated`. Excellent/good/fair/adequate are adequate; poor/inadequate are inadequate. A completed PEG regimen is not a grade. Boston adequacy requires every segment ≥2; a total ≥6 never overrides a segment scored 0 or 1. These reports always provide segments when Boston is used. The final post-lavage assessment controls over the initial view.
- `polyps` is the requested legacy alias for tissue acquisition, not literal polyp detection. It always equals `tissue_taken`. Either a polypectomy or a non-polyp biopsy means `yes`; a polyp left untouched means `no`. A lost resected polyp is still `yes` because polypectomy occurred, even though no specimen reached the laboratory.
- `histology` means a current colon tissue result is actually present. Prior colon results, same-day gastric results, lab receipt, and gross description do not count. An external result matched to this procedure and accession counts. A signed microscopic result counts while portal release is pending. No current tissue means `histology: no`.
- A later explicitly superseding correction controls over the original entry in either direction. A later final pathology addendum replaces pending status. Arabic clinical lines reflect that same final state. Superseded negative tissue wording is not interpreted as absence of tissue.
- Histology absence after an unretrieved polyp still maps to `awaiting_histology` under the specified task rules. This label does not promise that histology will ever become available. Likewise, `closed` is a task state, not a recommendation about further treatment or surveillance; a polyp left in place can meet this task's closure criteria.
- `hidden:undocumented_failure` identifies a true failure whose corresponding field is unstated. It is never a reason to infer failure from the report text. A model receiving text alone cannot uniquely recover latent truth in these cases.

Apply key-state precedence exactly:

| Priority | Documentary condition | key_state | key_reason |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | caecum = no | not_closed | incomplete_exam |
| 2 | prep = inadequate | not_closed | inadequate_prep |
| 3 | caecum or prep = not_stated | needs_human | missing_documentation |
| 4 | tissue_taken = yes and histology = no | not_closed | awaiting_histology |
| 5 | otherwise | closed | criteria_met |

Missing documentation therefore takes precedence over pending histology, while known incomplete extent or inadequate prep takes precedence over missing documentation. All output reports have exactly the requested fields: `uid`, `sha`, `text`, `truth`, `key_fields`, `key_state`, `key_reason`, `tags`. UIDs are `r001` through `r300`. Each `sha` hashes exactly the UTF-8 bytes of `text`, including its terminal newline.

## Verification and limitations

The generator checks all minimums, uniqueness, sequential UIDs, text hashes, field domains, alias equality, latent/documentary agreement where documented, expected scenario states, key precedence, Arabic and OCR tagging, correction tagging, and undocumented-failure tagging before writing output. Truth counts are asserted to be exactly 120/90/90. Output schema and rule precedence were also checked independently against the exported JSON.

Two consecutive actual generator runs were compared byte-for-byte. Two additional runs with different `PYTHONHASHSEED` values were also identical. All four runs passed. The fixed source date and sorted tag lists prevent clock and set-order variation. Reproducibility is verified in the supplied Python environment; exact seeded text across future Python versions would require rerunning the byte comparison.

This is a deliberately adversarial, template-derived evaluation fixture, not a representative sample of Abu Dhabi clinical practice, a prevalence estimate, or medical guidance. Arabic is a parallel clinical overlay, not a full Arabic-only report set; OCR noise is mild and does not test recovery of corrupted clinical scores. Clinical facts are invented from the task semantics, without external clinical validation. Scenario reuse and explicit attribution wording may make some examples easier than natural records. Review the decisions above before scoring a different clinical closure policy. Evaluate `key_fields` and text-based states separately from latent `truth`, and keep the answer key and generator away from the system being evaluated.
