## B. Gulf precedents: startup or SME pilot that became a contract

1. **BioTwin (Hub71, Canada).** This is the closest match. It joined Hub71 in 2024 and had DoH research approval. It then ran a breast-screening pilot with Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi (M42), announced in January 2025. That was followed by a **strategic partnership with Abu Dhabi Biobank (DoH and M42)** on 26 June 2026, starting with multi-cancer screening. No contract value was published.
   - Pilot: https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/health/cleveland-clinic-abu-dhabi-partners-with-biotwin-to-pilot-virtual-human-twin-technology-in-breast-cancer-screening/
   - Partnership: https://m42.ae/media-resources/news/abu-dhabi-biobank-and-biotwin-announce-strategic-partnership-to-expand-human-virtual-twin-technology-in-the-uae/
2. **Dubai Future Accelerators and DHA.** In cohort 1 (2016–17), 19 pilot agreements worth Dh120m were signed across 7 government entities. DHA picked 3 companies, but they weren't named. In cohort 4 (2018), DHA signed **MoUs** with Babylon, Bodyo, Admetsys and HINT Neuro. These are MoUs, and I found no follow-on procurement contract.
   - https://gulfnews.com/uae/government/future-accelerators-19-projects-worth-dh120m-signed-1.1943587
   - https://healthcare-digital.com/technology-and-ai/dubai-health-authority-signs-four-new-mous-accelerate-healthcare-innovation
3. **Malaffi/ADHDS.** The platform was built under a procured vendor contract (Orion Health, 2018–19), not a pilot. Later items are experiments or joint ventures, not conversions (MSD blockchain experiment; Nafas JV, April 2025). https://www.doh.gov.ae/en/news/doh-partnership-with-adhds-and-msd-gcc-to-experiment-with-the-application-of-blockchain-technology
4. **NABIDH and Riayati.** I found no public startup-to-contract news. Integration news covers only the authority platforms.

**Verdict:** I found **no clearly documented Gulf pilot-to-paid-contract conversion with a published value**. BioTwin is the best example of a pilot leading to a system-level agreement. DoH emirate-scale deals (Microsoft 2025, J&J 2026) went to large firms.
