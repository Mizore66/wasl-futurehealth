# Wasl: survival inputs and PPP/procurement routes

## 1. Stage-shift model inputs

**5-year survival by stage.** No UAE stage-specific figure was found.
- Saudi MNG-HA registry, 2009-17, n=1,012, CRC-specific: localized 79.9%, regional 63.3%, distant 20.3%. Use this as the base case. [PMC8390280](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8390280/)
- Saudi Cancer Registry, 1994-2004: 63.3%, 50.2% and 14.7% for the same stages. Vital status was known for only 5.6% of cases, so use it as a low bound. [BMC](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1186/1471-2458-13-73)
- Oman, SQUH: stages I/II/III at 100%, 60% and 60%. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26163603/)

**Stage at diagnosis (largely symptomatic).**
- MNG-HA: 33% distant; 22% localized. [PMC8390280](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC8390280/)
- Dubai, n=560: 31.6% metastatic. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41733163/)
- Oman: 42.6% stage III plus 32.7% stage IV, about 75% stage III/IV. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/26163603/)
- MENA registries: stage IV is 26-45% of staged cases. The UAE, Oman and Bahrain are lower than the rest of MENA; the UAE's own figure is paywalled. [IJC 2024](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/ijc.34895)
- Europe comparator: non-screen-detected cancers were 56.4% stage III-IV. [Lancet RH-Eur](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S2666776222001521)

**RCT mortality effects.**
- NordICC 2022, 10 years:
  - Intention-to-screen RR 0.90 (0.64-1.16).
  - Per-protocol mortality fell 50% (23-73%).
  - [WikiJournalClub](https://www.wikijournalclub.org/wiki/NordICC)
- NordICC 13-year update (2026): intention-to-screen RR 0.88; per-protocol RR 0.70 (0.26-1.25), not significant. [Healio](https://www.healio.com/news/gastroenterology/20260505/nordicc-trial-update-colonoscopy-reduced-crc-incidence-30-no-effect-on-mortality)
- Minnesota, 30 years: RR 0.68 annual and 0.78 biennial. Nottingham, 19.5 years: RR 0.91 (0.84-0.98). Funen, 17 years: RR 0.89 (0.78-1.01). [NCI PDQ](https://www.cancer.gov/types/colorectal/hp/colorectal-screening-pdq)
  - The Nottingham 2012 abstract reports a 13% reduction instead, so pick one. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/22052062/)
- Cochrane gFOBT:
  - Intention-to-screen RR 0.84 (0.78-0.90).
  - Attendance-adjusted RR 0.75 (0.66-0.84). This is the best proxy for a closed loop.
  - [Cochrane](https://www.cochrane.org/evidence/CD001216_screening-colorectal-cancer-using-faecal-occult-blood-test-hemoccult)
- In Minnesota, 83% of positives had a diagnostic workup. [PubMed](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/24047060/)

## 2. Malaffi/ADHDS and procurement routes

**PPP.**
- ADHDS was set up in 2019 as a project company under a PPP between DoH and Injazat (Mubadala). [PRN](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/orion-health-and-abu-dhabi-health-data-services-announce-partnership-to-deliver-the-first-health-information-exchange-in-the-middle-east-300785764.html)
- It is now an M42 company running Malaffi, Sahatna, e-Prescriptions and Shafafiya. [M42](https://m42.ae/what-we-do/digital-health-solutions/abu-dhabi-health-data-services/)
- No public text was found on the PPP's terms or on subcontracting.

**How vendors were engaged.** Each was contracted directly by ADHDS; no public tender was cited.
- **Orion:** "commercial agreement". [PRN](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/orion-health-and-abu-dhabi-health-data-services-announce-partnership-to-deliver-the-first-health-information-exchange-in-the-middle-east-300785764.html)
- **NextGate:** an EMPI component inside Orion's platform. [Orion](https://orionhealth.com/ca/media-releases/orion-health-delivers-the-first-health-information-exchange-hie-in-the-middle-east/)
- **VitalHub UK:** 5-year partnership, 2024. [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2024/02/20/2831719/0/en/VitalHub-UK-Expands-Healthtech-Portfolio-into-Middle-East-through-a-Partnership-with-ADHDS.html)
- **Philips:** reference-site agreement, 2024. [Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/health/department-of-health-abu-dhabi-partners-with-philips-to-establish-malaffi-platform-as-global-reference-resource/)
- **Digi7:** a UAE SME; collaboration announced October 2024. [Malaffi](https://www.malaffi.ae/what-is-malaffi/history-and-milestones/)
- **Inference:** partnering with ADHDS is the realistic route for Wasl.

**Startup vehicles.**
- **DoH HealthX** (with startAD, 2024): 30 startups; sandbox and de-identified data; contracts are only "potential". [DoH](https://www.doh.gov.ae/en/news/startad-and-doh-launch-healthx-to-advance-abu-dhabi-as-global-biotech-and-life-sciences-hub)
- **Hub71:**
  - Access Programme up to AED 750k.
  - Sandbox with ADDED.
  - BioTwin piloted at Cleveland Clinic Abu Dhabi after DoH approval.
  - [Hub71](https://www.hub71.com/impact/2025)
- **Not found:** a formal DoH pilot-to-procurement pathway.

**Procurement law.**
- **Abu Dhabi Procurement Standards** (mandatory from 1 July 2021): support for MSMEs and local content; no innovation clause. [Media Office](https://www.mediaoffice.abudhabi/en/government-affairs/dgs-launches-abu-dhabi-standards-for-government-procurement/)
- **Sole and single source:** "limited tender" types that need approval from the head of Procurement and under the Procurement Delegation of Authority. [Standards PDF](https://staticcdn.mbzuai.ac.ae/mbzuaiwpprd01/2024/07/Abu-Dhabi-Procurement-Standards.pdf)
- **MSME quota is 15%, not 10%** (April 2020, Ghadan 21). Bid bonds are suspended and guarantees waived up to AED 50m. [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/business/economy/abu-dhabi-earmarks-15-of-procurement-spending-to-smes-and-micro-businesses-1.1012424)
  - The OECD says the quota isn't tracked and the link between procurement and innovation is "underexplored". [OECD](https://www.oecd.org/content/dam/oecd/en/publications/reports/2023/05/assessment-of-policies-programmes-and-regulations-relating-to-msme-and-start-up-development-in-abu-dhabi_9c2d7886/9b92546e-en.pdf)
- **10% is the federal rule.** [u.ae](https://u.ae/en/information-and-services/business/Managing-and-growing-your-business/small-and-medium-enterprises)
- **"Law 3 of 2017": unverified. Don't cite it.**
- **PPP Law:** Abu Dhabi Law 2/2019 (ADIO), healthcare in scope. [Al Tamimi](https://www.tamimi.com/news/the-new-abu-dhabi-adio-and-ppp-laws/)
- **ICV certificate:** used in Abu Dhabi tender scoring. [ADDED](https://idb.added.gov.ae/en/Programs/ADLC/FAQs)
