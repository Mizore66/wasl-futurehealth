1.Standard Scope
1.1 Purpose:  
1.1.1 This document mandates the service specifications and data reporting requirements for 
DoH’s Periodic Comprehensive Screening Program for Adults in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi.  
1.1.2 It specifies the eligibility criteria, screening tests, frequency of testing and quality 
indicators to ensure that adults screened through the Periodic Comprehensive Screening 
Program receive quality, safe, effective, Participate -centered screening services and are 
referred in a timely manner for further assessment and appropriate management.  
1.2 Scope:  
1.2.1 These Service Specifications apply to all healthcare providers (facilities and professionals) 
licensed by DoH in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi who are participating in DoH’s Periodic 
Comprehensive Screening Program for Adults-IFHAS. 
1.3 These Service Specifications cover screening for the following chronic diseases:  
1.3.1 Cardiovascular disease (CVD) and risk factors such as Diabetes, Hypertension, 
        Dyslipidemia, Obesity and Smoking History.  
1.3.2 Reproductive Health Screening  
1.3.3 Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm screening. 
1.3.4 Cancer (Breast, Cervical, Lung & Colorectal) 
1.3.5 Bone Health (Osteoporosis) 
1.3.6 Mental Health (Depression & Anxiety Disorders) 
1.3.7 Oral Health 
1.3.8 Senior Citizen Health (Dementia, Vision and Hearing)

3.Standard Requirements and Specifications
All healthcare providers participating in DoH’s Periodic Comprehensive Screening Program for Adults 
in the Emirate of Abu Dhabi shall: 
3.1 Be licensed by DoH. 
3.2 Provide clinical services and Participate care in accordance with DoH policies and standards, and 
the laws and regulations of the Emirate of Abu Dhabi. 
3.3 Comply with relevant DoH Policies and Standards with special attention to: 
3.3.1 Policies and standards on Participants’ Education and General Consent: The licensed 
provider must provide appropriate education and information regarding the screening test and 
must ensure that appropriate General consent is obtained and documented on the 
participant’s medical record. 
3.3.2 Policies and standards on managing participants’ medical records include developing 
effective recording systems, maintaining records, confidentiality, privacy and secur ing the 
participants’ information; and educating them on services provided and satisfying the 
requirements of the informed consent and participants’ rights and responsibilities. 
3.3.3 DoH Regulations and Procedures, available at www.DoH.gov.ae  
3.4 Comply with DoH’s requests to inspect and audit records and cooperate with DoH authorized 
auditors as required by DoH. 
3.5 Comply with Information Security and Data Management requirements including sharing of IFHAS 
Questionnaire (refer to appendix 1), PHQ-9, GAD-7, Male reproductive health questionnaire 
(Appendix 2, female reproductive health questionnaire (Appendix 3) and other screening 
results/diagnosis and where applicable, pathology results, electronic participants’ records and 
disease management systems through Shafafiya platform.

category and obtained privileges: 
4.1.1 Perform Screening tests for the following conditions upon age and gender: 
4.1.2 
4.1.2.1 Cardiovascular diseases and risk factors (Diabetes, Hypertension, Dyslipidemia, Obesity, 
and Smoking History)  
4.1.2.2 Reproductive Health Screening 
4.1.2.3 Abdominal Aortic Aneurysm Screening 
4.1.2.4 Cancer (Breast, Cervical, Colorectal & Lung) 
4.1.2.5 Bone Health (Osteoporosis) 
4.1.2.1 Mental health (Depression & Anxiety Disorders) 
4.1.2.7 Oral Health. 
4.1.2.8 Senior Citizen Health (Dementia, Vision & Hearing) 
4.1.3 Follow up services and referrals for further assessment and management .

4.2 Facilities participating in DoH’s Periodic Comprehensive Screening Program for Adults should: 
4.2.1 Fulfill the duties of health care providers for the provision of the Periodic Comprehensive 
        Screening Program in accordance with Appendix 4 
4.2.2 Comply with DoH Periodic Comprehensive Screening Program Care Pathways, Screening 
measures and tests for the Comprehensive Screening Program, Assessment measure per age 
group, referral Pathway for abnormal results and Clinical  
        Quality Indicators and with Appendices 5, 6, 7 , 8 and 9 respectively. 
4.2.3 Assign a screening program coordinator who fulfills the role & responsibilities in  
        accordance with Appendix 4 
4.2.4 Maintain records of screening tests and outcomes. 
4.2.5 Establish internal audit procedures to demonstrate compliance with these service  
        requirements and other associated regulatory policies and standards . 
4.2.6 Ensure accessible service provision for all referred participants. 
4.2.7 Provide screening services during a wide range of times (weekdays, weekends, mornings  
        & evenings). 
4.2.8 Ensure that up-to-date lists of clinic-registered populations are maintained and are    
        accurate and complete. 
4.2.9 Ensure there is an adequate number of trained, qualified, and competent staff in place to  
        deliver high-quality screening services (who are competent and able to diagnose, interpret 
        finding and provide health education), in line with best practice guidelines and DoH  
         relevant standards. 
4.2.10 Ensure the relevant qualifications for the specific group of staff who will be delivering the 
services across the care pathway. However, all staff must demonstrate competence in their 
area. 
4.2.11 Ensure ongoing training of staff in the relevant standards/ guidelines, policies and 
specifications to keep them updated. 
4.2.12 Ensure the professional team carrying out the screening services include a minimum of 
physician, nurse and allied healthcare professional, registration clerk and a screening program 
care coordinator. 
4.2.13 Improve coverage and uptake by ensuring that invitations to the screening services are 
endorsed and sent on time; initial and follow-up appointments are offered, and appointment 
reminders are utilized. 
4.2.14 Deliver outreach activity services, if applicable, enhance the uptake fulfilling the 
requirements as availability of equipment and supplies needed for the screening tests and 
upon DoH policies and guidelines. 
4.2.15 On a case-by-case basis and subject to DoH’s Technical license Committee, the DoH may 
issue authorization for licensed healthcare providers other than licensed mobile units after 
passing ADPHC screening department auditing requirements to offer comprehensive screening 
in the workplace/home care/public places in accordance with the following:  
4.2.15.1  Licensed facilities should conduct campaigns to enhance access to 
comprehensive screening programs for eligible individuals. 
4.2.15.2 Healthcare provider scope of service meets the requirements specified in this 
standard and target workplace/public environment supports the requirements of this 
standard, specifically the ability to dedicate appropriate rooms that support privacy and 
confidentiality and that are compliant with the requirements of the relevant DoH policies 
and procedures.

4.3 Laboratories, Radiology and Audiology Services participating in DoH Periodic Comprehensive 
Screening Program for Adults must: 
4.3.1 Be licensed by DoH. 
4.3.2 Have in place systems, policies and operating procedures for laboratory services in 
accordance with the requirements of the DoH Clinical Laboratory Standards, and for 
radiological and audiology services if applicable, in addition to the requirement of DoH 
screening guidelines and standards. 
4.3.3 Laboratories must be accredited for health, safety and quality by a well-recognized 
international accreditation body. 
4.4 Healthcare professionals participating in the DoH Periodic Comprehensive Screening Program for 
Adults must: 
4.4.1 Be licensed by the DoH. 
4.4.2 Comply with the clinical standards detailed in this document to provide the most 
appropriate care, taking responsibility for deciding the best care options for managing 
screened cases and in accordance with Appendix 4 
4.4.3 Participating in Continuing Medical Education (CME) in accordance with DoH 
requirements. 
4.4.4 Comply with the relevant health care provider duties specified in Appendix 4. 
4.4.5 Meet regularly to discuss screening uptake and outcome activities, quality improvement 
and internal audit findings. 
4.5 Screening Participants Eligibility Criteria: 
4.5.1 Member of the THIQA program 
4.5.2 18 years old and above. 
4.5.3 Female or male based on the specific Screening Program eligibility criteria.  
4.6 Test Measures/Frequency of the Service Provision: 
4.6.1 The screening tools and the frequency of screening per age group for each screened 
medical condition of the program are discussed in Appendix 6 and 7. 
4.7 Recruitment of Screening Participates:  
The Provider of the Periodic Comprehensive Screening Program for Adults shall have: 
4.7.1 An “Invitation System” for the initiation of the first screening, rescreening and recall for 
positive and insufficient results. 
4.7.2 A system in place to allow for: 
4.7.2.1. Referral from health care professionals, relevant stakeholders or self-referral.  
4.7.2.2. Onward referrals to other relevant licensed health care providers were appropriate. 
4.8 Periodic Comprehensive Screening Pathway: 
4.8.1 Activities prior to screening: 
To ensure appropriate and efficient use of health resources in accordance with good management 
practice and experience, the program should: 
4.8.1.1 Identify and invite eligible participants for the screening at appropriate time and 
intervals outside of the clinic-registered population. 
Page 7 of 24 
4.8.1.2 Provide the participants with the information required, so they can make an 
informed decision on whether to participate. 
4.8.2 During the initial visit, the providers of the program should: 
4.8.2.1 Ensure that the IFHAS participants’ informed consents are obtained and 
documented. 
4.8.2.2 Ensure IFHAS participants are provided with appropriate counselling, education 
and information regarding the screening tests, assessment, follow-up care.  
4.8.2.3 For a married female participant in childbearing age inquire regards plans to 
conceive. Consider referring to preconception clinic for case assessment and tailored 
counseling. 
4.9 With respect to follow-up visits, all healthcare providers of the Periodic Comprehensive 
Screening Program for Adults must: 
4.9.1 Schedule follow up visits and inform participants who have had completed/partially 
completed the Comprehensive Screening Program at the time of the screening about their 
appointments to receive the screening results in the case of abnormal results, appropriate 
counselling and referrals. Teleconsultation option can be utilized (refer to Tele consultation 
Standard). The ordering physician is responsible for communicating back to participants on 
their screening results especially if they do not show up for follow up. 
4.9.2 Ensure the following when communicating the screening tests results to the participants:  
4.9.2.1 Physicians should ensure proper documentation when communicating results to 
participants during follow-up visit (in person visit/teleconsultation visit) within 10 days of 
the initial visit. 
4.9.2.2 If the test outcome is normal, the screened participant is discharged to routine 
screening as per the frequency mentioned in this document. 
4.9.2.3 If the test outcome is abnormal, the provider should ensure that the participant 
is physically present when discussing the findings and receiving the appropriate 
counselling.  
4.9.3 Referrals shall be made if IFHAS abnormal results noted (Appendix 8), if needed, to other 
appropriately licensed and privileged health professionals within a period specified in 
accordance with the DoH Care Pathway of each standard or program Service Specification for 
the provision of screening services. 
4.9.4 Establish a network of secondary care with specific conditions to facilitate the candidate’s 
journey and to receive timely and complete feedback on referrals. 
4.10  Information on Screening and Educational Material: 
4.10.1 At relevant points during the screening pathway, participating individuals should be 
provided with appropriate information on each type of screening delivered by the Periodic 
Comprehensive Screening Program for Adults.  
4.10.2 A trained interpreter should be available during appointments, if needed, along with 
appropriate written information. 
4.10.3 Individuals participating from People of determination (PoD) should be provided with 
support to enable them to understand all processes and results . 
4.10.4 Telephone and email queries in relation to the Periodic Comprehensive Screening 
Program for Adults activities should be dealt with promptly.

4.10.4.1 Participating in education and information regarding available services should be 
those provided by ADPHC or developed with the involvement of ADPHC. 
4.10.4.2 Providers must always use the educational material developed by ADPHC for all 
stages of the screening pathway to ensure accurate messages about the risks and benefits 
of screening and about any subsequent surveillance or treatment provided .  
4.10.4.3 Providers must inform and coordinate with ADPHC prior development any other

4.11 Results Reporting: 
Healthcare facilities providing the Periodic Comprehensive Screening Program for Adults 
should:  
4.11.1 Report and submit data to DoH via e-claims in accordance with the DoH Reporting of 
Health Statistics standards and as set out in the DoH Data Standards and Procedures (available 
at Reporting - Shafafiya | Department of Health Abu Dhabi).  
4.11.2 Ensure that the data entered is accurate and is validated prior to submitting to DoH. 
4.11.3 Data entry validation remains the responsibility of the healthcare professional responsible 
for screening; data entered by registration clerk must not be changed or edited without the 
approval of the healthcare professional responsible for the screening.  
4.11.4 In addition, to reporting through the “E-claims system” providers of the Periodic 
Comprehensive Screening Program are expected to ensure: 
4.11.4.1 The clerk responsible for registering the participant should tag the participant for 
IFHAS to allow tracking through Malaffi and results tag in Sahatna. 
4.11.4.2 Collect and circulate routine activity data, outcome data, and statistics, and 
monitor the services against DoH’s published standards/guidelines. 
4.11.4.3 The participant journey is completed and the results of screening data reported 
through Shafafiya system within window of 15 days from the initial visit.   If IFHAS 
screening package is not completed, then claims cannot be submitted.  
4.11.4.4 Report Key Performance Indicators as per Appendix 4 within a maximum of 21 
days after the end of each quarter. 
5.Monitoring and Evaluation
All providers of the Periodic Comprehensive Screening Program for Adults are required to submit and report 
their performances and should be evaluated in accordance with the following: 
5.1 Statistical data and accuracy of reporting including coverage and uptake performance.  
5.2 Auditing performed by DoH or third party.  
5.3 Key Performance Indicators are set out in Appendix 5.

Appendix 4: Health care Provider’s Duties 
Service Care Coordinator 
• Ensuring that IFHAS registration is done correctly and efficiently.
• Guiding participants through their screening journey with clarity and support.
• Educating and reassuring participants about the importance of screening and early
detection.
• Maintaining high standards by checking that your facility meets DoH audit
requirements.
• Monitoring and supervising your teams, ensuring they are well-trained and aligned
with IFHAS objectives.
• Providing ADPHC with accurate monthly uptake feedback and quarterly KPI
submissions, allowing us to track our progress and improve services.
• Ensuring that all IFHAS materials (such as vests and participants’ information sheets)
are available and properly used.
Service Physician 
• Provides clinical services and participant care in accordance with this document.
• Provides appropriate participant education regarding the services and the screening
tests.
• Ensures that participant consent has been obtained and is documented in
accordance with DoH policies.
• Performs age-appropriate assessment, complete physical exam and counselling/
anticipatory guidance/risk factor reduction interventions.
• Orders screening tests (Appendix 3b), follow up results and communicate results to
all participants.
• Referring abnormal cases to appropriate specialties (Appendix 8).
• For abnormal female or male reproductive health screening to follow the clinical
pathway (Appendices 2 & 3).
• Complies with the regulation on managing medical records and Participate
information as per DoH policies.
• Participate in training the team to ensure smooth working flow.
• Physicians working with the IFHAS program should obtain CMEs related to the
screenings to be up to date with the service.
Service Nurse 
• Ensure that an appropriate participant general consent is obtained, filled and
signed.
• Ensure that the participant completes the IFHAS health questionnaire.
• Ensure preparation of the participant files and appointment list in coordination with
the receptionist.
• Performs and documents the participator’s vital signs and measurements as per the
specific screening service provided.
• Nurses working with the IFHAS program should obtain CMEs related to the screenings to be up to date.
